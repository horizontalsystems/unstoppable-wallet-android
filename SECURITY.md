## What it is

F.L.Y Security exists to put real de-escalation knowledge directly into people's hands. No badge required.

## Why this exists
Certified de-escalation training exists. It's taught to police. It's often not used â€” replaced instead with a show of force or authority that escalates situations that didn't need to escalate.

This gap costs people. It costs Indigenous communities and other over-policed communities disproportionately, and it's documented, not anecdotal.

F.L.Y Security starts from a simple idea: if the people who are supposed to de-escalate won't always do it, the people who are most exposed should have the same tools anyway.

## Who it's for
- People who want to know their rights and de-escalation tactics before they're in a tense situation, not during one
- Communities who've historically had reason not to trust that a call for help will be answered safely
- Anyone who wants practical, plain-language training â€” not legal jargon, not a 40-hour academy course
